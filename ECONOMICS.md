# Unit Economics

Credex is positioned as a high-intent B2B lead generator that can monetize through paid audits, consulting, or subscription-based spend monitoring. The primary economic lever is converting a free audit into a paid engagement for procurement optimization or credit-based discounts.

Assumptions:
- Target customer size: 5-200 employees, 3-8 AI tools, $1,000-$20,000 monthly AI spend.
- Average identified savings per audit: $450/month ($5,400/year).
- Paid offer: $1,200 one-time optimization package or $99/month monitoring plan.
- Gross margin: 85% for the subscription, 70% for services.

Lead value estimation:
- Free audit to lead capture: 20% (e.g., 20 leads from 100 audits).
- Lead to call booked: 30% (6 calls from 20 leads).
- Call to paid conversion: 25% (1.5 customers from 6 calls).
- Effective conversion from audit to paid: 1.5%.

If 1,000 audits are completed in a month, expected paid conversions are ~15. At a $1,200 one-time package, that is $18,000 revenue in month one. If half of those choose a subscription at $99/month instead, MRR becomes ~$742.5 and grows with each cohort. The unit economics improve further as paid traffic scales and referral loops increase organic audits.

CAC modeling:
- Early stage acquisition uses founder-led outbound and communities: CAC ~$20-$40 per completed audit (primarily time cost).
- Paid social / search test: $3 CPC, 8% landing-to-audit conversion, 20% lead capture; CAC per lead ~$187; CAC per paid customer ~$12,467 at 1.5% conversion. This is too high without optimization.
- With improved conversion to 3% and 30% lead capture, CAC per paid customer drops to ~$3,333. Combined with a $1,200 one-time package, this is not viable unless LTV increases (subscription or upsell).

Therefore, initial strategy focuses on organic and outbound channels where CAC stays below $200 per lead, and conversion to paid is 10-20% on high-intent accounts. In that scenario, CAC per paid customer falls to ~$1,000, which is covered by the $1,200 package and creates a margin buffer for retention or upsell.

$1M ARR path:
- 850 customers on the $99/month monitoring plan = ~$1,009,800 ARR.
- If conversion to subscription is 2% of audits, then 42,500 audits per year are needed. This is feasible with distribution through communities, integrations, and referral sharing if the public audit links drive virality.

Profitability math:
- Subscription gross margin: 85% (infra costs are low; LLM summaries can be capped and cached).
- At $99/month and 850 customers, gross profit is ~$858k/year.
- With a small team and $250k annual operating costs, net profit target is reachable once the customer base scales.
