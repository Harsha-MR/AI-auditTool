# Go-To-Market Plan

Credex targets founders, engineering leads, and finance or ops managers at startups with 5-200 employees who already pay for multiple AI tools but lack centralized visibility. The primary pain is not choosing tools, it is knowing whether the current tool mix and seat allocation is rational. The audit delivers a concrete monthly savings number and a defensible explanation, which makes it easy to share internally and start a cost optimization conversation.

The ideal first customer is a seed to Series B startup using 3-6 AI tools across engineering and GTM teams. They typically manage spend via a shared credit card or ad-hoc reimbursement. They care about efficiency but do not have a formal procurement process. This segment values fast, frictionless insights more than heavy integrations, so a form-based audit and instant report are the right starting point.

User behavior to leverage: these teams already compare AI tool pricing on Reddit, X, and product hunt comments; they track cost per seat in Notion or spreadsheets; and they tend to share tools internally when a new tool looks better. Credex uses that natural sharing behavior by producing a public, redacted audit link and a summary that can be forwarded to stakeholders.

Distribution plan for the first 100 users:
1) Founder-led outbound to 40-60 seed and Series A startups with public AI tool usage (job posts, stack references, or public tech stacks). The outreach offers a free audit with a 48-hour turnaround and a shareable report.
2) Communities where early adopters discuss AI tooling spend: r/startups, r/Entrepreneur, r/saas, r/technology, and engineering manager groups on Slack (Rands, Eng Managers, and AI Ops communities). The hook is a sample report with real savings numbers and a call to run a 5-minute audit.
3) Productized audits on LinkedIn and X: short threads showing common overspend patterns (unused seats, enterprise plans for small teams, duplicated tools) and a CTA for a free audit.

The unfair advantage is the combination of pricing research, audit heuristics, and immediate, defensible reasoning. Most competitors either bury pricing logic or ask for an email before showing value. Credex shows value first, which increases trust and makes the lead capture more likely to convert.

Week-1 traction metrics:
- 20 completed audits
- 8 shareable links created
- 5 lead captures
- 2 follow-up calls scheduled

Retention loop: when pricing changes, Credex can notify existing leads with a refreshed audit showing updated savings. This creates a recurring reason to re-engage, and positions Credex as a “pricing change monitor” rather than a one-time calculator.
